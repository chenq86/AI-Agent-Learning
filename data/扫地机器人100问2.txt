# Frequently Asked Questions and Answers for Robot Vacuums (100 items)

### Basic Usage

1. **What should I do when using the robot vacuum for the first time?**

- Remove all packaging accessories from the body, fully charge it, start mapping in an open environment, and set cleaning zones and no-go areas after completion.

2. **What if the robot does not move after turning on?**

- Check if the body is tangled with bumper strips or power cords, ensure the caster wheels and drive wheels at the bottom are not stuck, then restart the device and try again.

3. **How to handle the situation where the robot cannot find the charging dock?**

- Clear obstacles around the charging dock, ensuring there are no obstructions within 3 meters in front and 1 meter to the left and right of the dock, place the robot near the dock and restart auto-charging.

4. **What if the APP cannot connect to the robot?**

- Ensure the phone and robot are connected to the same 2.4G WiFi (5G is currently not supported), restart the robot and router, and re-add the device in the APP.

5. **How to resolve incomplete mapping or chaotic maps?**

- Clean reflective objects in the environment (such as mirrors, polished floor tiles) and moving obstacles, delete the old map, and let the robot remap.

6. **Why does the robot frequently pause during cleaning?**

- Check if the dust bin and filter are clogged, if the battery is low, or if the body sensors are covered by dust; clean them and try again.

7. **How to set up scheduled cleaning?**

- Open the companion APP, go to [Cleaning Settings] - [Scheduled Cleaning], select the cleaning time, area, and frequency, save it, and the robot will execute automatically.

8. **What if the robot returns to charge immediately after turning on?**

- Check the remaining battery level; if it is below 20%, it will prioritize returning to charge. If the battery is sufficient, restart the device and reset the cleaning mode.

9. **What if manual remote control of the robot has no response?**

- Confirm that the phone and robot are connected properly, exit and re-enter the APP, check if the robot is in "Auto Cleaning" mode, and switch to "Manual Mode".

10. **The robot does not automatically return to charge after cleaning is complete?**

- Check if the "Auto Return to Charge" function is disabled in the APP, confirm that the charging dock position in the map has not been modified, and clean dust from the robot's return-to-charge sensors.

### Cleaning Effectiveness

11. **What if there is still dust and debris on the floor after cleaning?**

- Check if the main brush and side brushes are worn or tangled with hair, clean the dust bin and filter, set suction to the highest level, and increase cleaning frequency.

12. **How to solve missed areas during cleaning?**

- Check if restricted zones have been set; try remapping or manually remote-control the robot to cover the missed area.

13. **What to do if corners and edges are not cleaned thoroughly?**

- Check if the side brush rotates normally and is not worn; switch the robot to "Edge Cleaning" mode and manually assist in cleaning corner dead spots.

14. **How to handle incomplete dust removal on carpets?**

- Confirm that the robot supports carpet boost function, set suction to maximum, clear debris from the carpet surface, and prevent the robot from slipping.

15. **What to do if water stains or marks remain on the floor after mopping?**

- Reduce the water tank output, replace with a clean mop cloth, change the mopping mode to "Dry Mop" or "Low-Speed Mop", and avoid the robot staying repeatedly in the same area.

16. **How to solve remaining dirt on the floor after mopping?**

- Check if the mop cloth is dirty or hardened; wash or replace it promptly. For stubborn stains, manually spray cleaner and let the robot re-mop.

17. **What to do if the robot misses hair during cleaning?**

- Clean hair tangled around the main brush, replace with a pet-specific anti-tangle main brush, set suction to high level, and enable "Hair Cleaning Mode".

18. **What to do if large particles (such as cookie crumbs) cannot be sucked in?**

- Check if the roller brush chamber is blocked, ensure the intake is unobstructed, switch the robot to "High Suction Mode", and prevent large particles from jamming the roller brush.

19. **How to handle inconsistent cleaning results on different floor types?**

- Set up "Zone Cleaning" in the APP, adjust suction and mopping modes separately for carpets and tiles, and optimize cleaning parameters.

20. **What to do if lint from the mop cloth remains on the floor after cleaning?**

- Replace with a new low-lint mop cloth, soak and wring it dry before use to prevent shedding, and lightly wipe the floor with a dry cloth after cleaning.

### Consumables and Maintenance

21. **How often should the main brush be replaced?**

- Under daily use, replace standard main brushes every 3-6 months, and every 1-3 months for households with pets. Replace immediately if wear or deformation occurs.

22. **What is the impact of worn side brushes? How to replace them?**

- Worn side brushes lead to incomplete edge cleaning. Remove the side brush clips at the bottom of the body, directly install a new side brush, and press firmly until locked.

23. **Can the HEPA filter be washed and reused?**

- The filter can be rinsed with clean water monthly, dried in a cool place before use, and must not be exposed to direct sunlight. It is recommended to replace it with a new filter after 3-6 months of use.

24. **How often should the dust bin be cleaned?**

- For ordinary households, clean it every 2-3 days. For households with pets or large apartments, it is recommended to clean it daily to avoid overflow affecting suction performance.

25. **How to wash and replace the mop cloth?**

- The mop cloth can be washed with water and reused repeatedly. Wash it promptly when it becomes dirty or stiff. Replace it with a new mop cloth after 1-2 months of use to ensure mopping effectiveness.

26. **What to do if battery life becomes shorter?**

- Avoid letting the robot sit for long periods with low charge; return it to the charging dock promptly after each use. Regularly perform full charge and discharge cycles to calibrate the battery. If severe degradation occurs after 1-2 years of use, consider replacing the battery.

27. **How often should the dust bag be replaced?**

- For robots with automatic dust collection, replace the dust bag every 1-2 months for ordinary households, and every half month to 1 month for households with pets.

28. **What are the symptoms of a clogged filter? How to clean it?**

- A clogged filter will cause reduced suction power and abnormal noises from the robot. Remove the filter, gently brush off dust with a soft-bristled brush, rinse with clean water, dry it, and reinstall it.

29. **How to solve caster wheel sticking or noise?**

- Use tweezers to remove hair and threads tangled around the caster wheels. Apply a small amount of lubricant to the pivot point to prevent sticking that affects movement.

30. **What to do if drive wheels slip or stop turning?**

- Clean debris and hair from the gaps in the drive wheels. Check if the tires are worn out; replace them promptly if severely worn to prevent the robot from being unable to move.

31. **What to do if the water tank leaks?**

- Check if the water tank cap is tightened securely and if the outlet pipe is blocked. Reduce the water flow setting. Avoid vigorous shaking of the robot during mopping. Replace the water tank if it is damaged.

32. **Can cleaning solution be added arbitrarily?**

- It is recommended to use cleaning solutions specifically designed for the robot. Do not add corrosive liquids such as alcohol, disinfectants, or dish soap, to avoid damaging the water tank and floors.

33. **How to remove odors from the roller brush compartment?**

- Remove the roller brush and the compartment, wash them with clean water, and let them dry. Spray a small amount of pet-specific deodorizer. Clean regularly to prevent mold growth.

34. **How to store the battery if not used for a long time?**

- Charge the battery to 50%-70%, remove it, and store it in a cool, dry place. Recharge it every 1-2 months to avoid damage from deep discharge.

35. **How to clean hair tangled in the main brush?**

- Remove the clips at both ends of the main brush, take out the main brush, cut off the tangled hair with scissors, and use a brush to clear debris from the roller brush compartment.

### Fault Alarm Category

36. **How to handle abnormal alarm prompts?**

- Check the error code; common issues have solution guides in the APP.

37. **How to handle the "Dust bin not installed" alarm when the dust bin is already installed?**

- Check if the dust bin is properly seated, clean dust from the dust bin sensor, reinstall the dust bin and secure the clips.

38. **How to resolve the "Filter clogged" alarm?**

- Immediately remove the filter, clean dust and debris from the filter and suction port, wash the filter and let it dry before reinstalling, then restart the robot.

39. **How to handle the "Battery fault" alarm?**

- Turn off the robot's power, unplug and replug the battery. If the alarm persists, contact after-sales service for battery inspection and replace the battery if necessary.

40. **What to do about the "Sensor anomaly" alarm?**

- Wipe all sensors on the front and bottom of the body with a dry soft cloth to avoid obstruction by dust or water stains, then restart the device after cleaning.

41. **How to resolve the "Collision sensor fault" alarm?**

- Check if the bumper strip is stuck or deformed, gently press the bumper strip by hand to confirm normal rebound, clean dust from the sensor, and restart to retry.

42. **How to handle the "Mopping system fault" alarm?**

- Check if the water tank is properly installed and if the mop is stuck, clear debris from the mopping module, restart the robot. If the alarm persists, contact after-sales service.

43. **What causes the robot to emit a "beep beep beep" alarm sound?**

- Mostly due to low battery, clogged consumables, or sensor anomalies. Check the body indicator lights and APP prompts to address the specific issue.

44. **What to do about the "Recharge sensor fault" alarm?**

- Wipe the recharge sensor on the bottom of the robot, clean the infrared sensing area of the charging dock to ensure no obstructions, and try recharging again.

45. **How to handle the "Roller brush fault" alarm?**

- Remove the roller brush, clean tangled hair and debris, check if the roller brush motor is jammed, reinstall the roller brush, and restart.

46. **How to solve the issue where the robot alarms and shuts down immediately after powering on?**

- Check the body for short circuits or abnormal noises, immediately disconnect the power, contact after-sales service to inspect the motor and circuit, do not disassemble it yourself.

47. **What to do if there is a "WiFi module fault" alarm?**

- Restart the robot and router, restore the robot to factory settings, reconnect WiFi, if the alarm persists contact after-sales service to replace the WiFi module.

48. **How to resolve a "anti-drop sensor fault" alarm?**

- Wipe the anti-drop sensors on the bottom of the robot with a dry cloth to avoid dust obstruction, check if the sensors are damaged, contact after-sales service if necessary.

49. **How to handle a "side brush fault" alarm?**

- Check if the side brush is tangled with debris or properly installed, remove the side brush for cleaning and reinstall it, contact after-sales service if the motor does not rotate.

50. **What to do if there is a "dust collection system fault" alarm (automatic dust collection models)?**

- Check if the dust bag is properly installed or full, clear debris from the dust collection channel, restart the dust collection system, if the alarm persists contact after-sales service.

### Feature-Specific Issues

51. **What to do if the anti-drop function fails?**

- Wipe the anti-drop sensors on the bottom, check if the sensors are obstructed, place the robot on flat ground, restart the device to calibrate the sensors.

52. **How to solve poor obstacle avoidance that frequently collides with furniture?**

- Clean the obstacle avoidance camera and sensors at the front of the body to avoid dust or water stain obstruction, enable "Advanced Obstacle Avoidance Mode" in the APP, rebuild the map.

53. **How to handle the carpet boost function not working?**

- Confirm the robot supports the carpet boost function, enable this function in the APP, check if the carpet is too thin for the robot to recognize.

54. **How to solve the issue where zones cannot be selected for zone cleaning?**

- Confirm the map building is complete, delete the old map and rebuild it, update the APP and the robot's internal system, try selecting zones again after restarting.

55. **What to do if the robot still enters restricted areas after setting them?**

- Check if the boundary of the restricted area is set accurately, readjust the range of the restricted area, save and let the robot re-recognize the map, disable the "Temporary Disable Restricted Area" function.

56. **How to handle the automatic dust collection function not working?**

- Check if the dust bag is properly installed, if the dust bin is closed, clean the dust collection channel, confirm the connection between the robot and the dust collection station is normal.

57. **What to do if voice control function has no response?**

- Confirm the robot is connected to a voice assistant (such as Xiao Ai Tongxue, Tmall Genie), check network connection, rebind the voice device, increase the volume of voice commands.

58. **How to resolve when mopping mode cannot be enabled?**

- Check if the water tank is properly installed and contains water, ensure the mop cloth is correctly attached, confirm that the mopping function is not disabled in the APP, and restart the robot.

59. **What to do if the robot deviates from the wall edge in edge-cleaning mode?**

- Wipe the edge sensors on the body, check if the side brush is worn out, place the robot on a flat surface, and restart the edge-cleaning mode.

60. **How to handle inaccurate spot cleaning function?**

- Confirm that the map building is complete, recalibrate the spot cleaning location in the APP, clear obstacles in that area, and let the robot reposition itself.

61. **What to do if the robot fails to cross thresholds?**

- Confirm that the threshold height is within the robot's crossing range (generally ≤2cm), clear debris at the threshold, manually assist the robot to cross once so it can memorize the position.

62. **What to do if cleaning records cannot be viewed in the APP?**

- Check the network connection, update the APP to the latest version, restart the robot, and confirm that the "Upload Cleaning Records" feature is enabled on the robot.

63. **How to resolve inability to switch between multi-floor maps?**

- Confirm that the robot supports multi-floor mapping, build maps for each floor separately in the APP, clean the identification markers between floors, and try switching after restarting.

64. **What to do if suction power decreases in silent mode?**

- Silent mode defaults to lower suction power to reduce noise. If high suction is needed, turn off silent mode and select "Standard Suction" or "High Suction".

65. **What to do if the robot's cleaning path becomes chaotic during operation?**

- Clear moving obstacles in the environment, delete the old map and rebuild it, enable "Planned Cleaning" mode, and avoid random cleaning.

### Special Scenarios and Usage

66. **How to optimize cleaning performance in households with pets?**

- Replace with an anti-tangle main brush and high-density filter, enable "Pet Mode", increase suction power and cleaning frequency, and clean the dust bin and roller brush daily.

67. **How to plan cleaning for large houses?**

- Use "Zone Cleaning" to clean different areas in batches. If the robot's battery life is insufficient, set up "Breakpoint Resume Cleaning" so it automatically returns to charge after completion.

68. **What should be noted when mopping wooden floors?**

- Reduce the water output of the tank, use dry mopping mode, avoid water pooling that may damage the floor, wring out the mop cloth before use, and promptly clean up water stains on the floor.

69. **How to avoid water stains on tiled floors?**

- Replace with a mop cloth that has good water absorption, reduce the water output, let the robot mop at low speed, and open doors and windows for ventilation after cleaning to speed up floor drying.

70. **What should families with children pay attention to when using a robot?**

- Set up exclusive no-go zones in children's rooms to avoid the robot colliding with toys, place the robot in a location out of children's reach for charging, and regularly check consumables.

71. **What cleaning mode is suitable for renters and small apartments?**

- Enable "One-Click Cleaning" or "Mini Cleaning" mode, set scheduled cleaning, clean for short periods daily to ensure floors remain clean.

72. **Can the robot clean pet cat litter and dog litter?**

- It can clean lightly scattered cat and dog litter; switch to high suction mode, avoid large-grain cat litter getting stuck in the roller brush, and empty the dustbin promptly.

73. **How to clean oil stains on kitchen floors?**

- First enable high suction mode to clear debris from the oil stain surface, then use dedicated cleaning solution combined with mopping mode for heavy mopping; manually pre-treat oil stains if necessary.

74. **What should be noted when cleaning bathroom floors?**

- Avoid large amounts of standing water on bathroom floors, clean water stains at bathroom thresholds, enable anti-drop functionality to prevent the robot from falling into toilets or floor drains.

75. **What to do if the robot cannot move on long-pile carpets?**

- Confirm the robot supports long-pile carpet cleaning, switch to high suction mode, clear debris from the carpet surface, avoid the roller brush tangling with long fibers, and disable carpet boost if necessary.

76. **How to resolve mapping errors near mirrors and glass?**

- Set no-go zones near mirrors and glass, or cover mirrors with blackout cloth to prevent reflections from affecting sensors, and rebuild the map.

77. **What to do if furniture bottom clearance is too low for the robot to enter?**

- Measure the height under the furniture to confirm if it falls within the robot's accessible range; if it cannot enter, manually clean under the furniture or switch to an ultra-thin model robot.

78. **Can the robot clean under beds and sofas?**

- If the height under beds and sofas is ≥ the robot's height, enable "Deep Cleaning" mode to clean them; remove tangled objects underneath beforehand and set no restricted areas.

79. **What should be noted when using the robot in winter?**

- Avoid letting the robot operate for extended periods in low-temperature environments, as battery life will decrease in cold conditions; return to charge promptly and clear frost from the body surface.

80. **What should be noted when using the robot in summer?**

- Avoid letting the robot operate in direct sunlight, regularly clean motor heat dissipation vents to prevent overheating protection, and refill the water tank promptly.

### Upgrades and After-Sales Service

81. **How to upgrade the robot's internal system?**

- After connecting to WiFi, the APP will prompt a system update. Click "Upgrade Now". During the upgrade, keep the robot powered on and connected to the network. Do not cut off power.

82. **Can the robot be reset to factory settings? How to operate it?**

- Yes. In the APP, go to [Settings] - [Device Info] - [Restore Factory Settings], or long-press the Power button + Return-to-Charge button on the body for 10 seconds. After restoration, you need to rebuild the map and reconfigure settings.

83. **What to do if you forget the APP login password?**

- On the APP login page, click "Forgot Password", enter the bound mobile phone number, obtain the verification code, and then reset the password.

84. **How to reconnect the robot after changing phones?**

- Unbind the robot in the old phone's APP. Download the same APP on the new phone, log in to your account, add the device again, and connect to WiFi.

85. **How to reuse the robot after moving house?**

- Delete the old map, clear obstacles in the new home environment, rebuild the map, set up new cleaning areas and no-go zones, and check if consumables need replacement.

86. **What to do if the robot malfunctions after the warranty expires?**

- Contact the brand's official after-sales service for paid repair, or choose a third-party professional repair agency. Do not disassemble the robot yourself.

87. **How to check the robot's warranty period?**

- Check the robot's product manual, purchase invoice, or view the warranty time in the APP under [Device Info]. Generally, the whole machine is warranted for 1-2 years, and the battery for 6 months.

88. **What to do if the robot cannot connect after changing the WiFi name or password?**

- Long-press the WiFi reset button on the robot body to restore WiFi settings. Search for the new WiFi in the APP, enter the password, and connect.

89. **Where to buy robot accessories?**

- You can buy original accessories at the brand's official flagship store, APP mall, or offline authorized stores. Avoid using non-original accessories that may damage the robot.

90. **How to determine the cause of abnormal noises from the robot?**

- A "hissing sound" usually indicates a clogged filter; a "clicking sound" often means debris tangled in the roller brush or side brush; a "humming sound" typically suggests motor failure. Clean accordingly or contact after-sales service.

91. **Can I disassemble and repair the robot myself?**

- Not recommended. The robot's internal circuits and sensors are complex. Self-disassembly will damage the equipment and void the warranty. Contact professional after-sales service upon malfunction.

92. **Does paint peeling or cosmetic damage affect the robot's usage?**

- Purely cosmetic damage or paint peeling does not affect core functions. If it affects aesthetics, you can contact after-sales service to replace the shell. If collision causes internal component damage, have it inspected promptly.

93. **How to resolve APP crashes and lag?**

- Close the APP background processes, update the APP to the latest version, clear phone memory; if it still crashes, uninstall and reinstall the APP.

94. **How to bind multiple phones to control the same robot?**

- In the main phone's APP, go to [Device Sharing], invite other phones to log in with the same account, or share the device via phone number; after acceptance, they can jointly control it.

95. **What to do if the robot's cleaning area does not match the annotation?**

- Confirm that map building is complete, clear environmental obstacles, re-calibrate the cleaning area in the APP, and check whether the robot has missed areas or cleaned repeatedly.

96. **What to do if the robot cannot turn on in low-temperature environments?**

- Move the robot to a room-temperature environment (above 5°C), let it rest for 30 minutes, recharge it, and avoid using it in low-temperature environments.

97. **How to handle severe heating during robot charging?**

- Check if the charging dock has good ventilation, avoid charging the robot in enclosed environments, pause charging, wait for the body to cool down before resuming charging; if it still heats up, contact after-sales service.

98. **How to extend the service life of the robot vacuum?**

- Regularly clean consumables and sensors, replace worn parts in time, avoid bumping or dropping the robot, properly store the battery when not in use for long periods, and perform regular startup maintenance.

99. **Are accessories from different brands interchangeable?**

- Interchangeability is not recommended; accessories from different brands have different sizes and specifications, and using non-original accessories may affect cleaning performance or even damage the robot.

100. **How to solve reduced suction power after using the robot for some time?**

- Thoroughly clean dust and debris from the dustbin, filter, roller brush, and suction port, replace worn main brushes and side brushes, calibrate the motor; if suction remains insufficient, contact after-sales service for inspection.
