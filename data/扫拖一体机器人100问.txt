# Common Questions and Answers for Robot Vacuums with Mopping Function (100 New Entries)

### Integrated Sweeping and Mopping Functions

1. **Can a robot vacuum with mopping function sweep only without mopping?**

- Yes, enter [Sweeping Mode] in the companion APP, turn off "Mopping Function", and keep only the suction mode to sweep separately.

2. **How to set up a cleaning process that sweeps first then mops?**

- Open the APP, select "Sweep First Then Mop" mode in [Custom Cleaning], customize sweeping suction power and mopping water output, and the robot will automatically execute according to the process.

3. **Will the robot smear stains if it sweeps while mopping?**

- No, the sweep-first mode cleans floor debris and dust before mopping. You can also set "Sweep-Mop Zoning" in the APP to avoid stain spreading.

4. **Can the mopping function be enabled separately?**

- Yes, clean the robot's dust bin, install the water tank and mop cloth, select "Mop Only" mode in the APP, and adjust water output as needed.

5. **Does suction power decrease when sweeping and mopping simultaneously?**

- Some models default to slightly adjusting suction power to match the mopping rhythm. You can manually set suction to high level in the APP to achieve high-power simultaneous sweeping and mopping.

6. **How to set different parameters for sweeping and mopping in various areas?**

- After map creation is complete, name the zones on the map, and set suction power, water output, and cleaning frequency individually for each area in [Zone Settings].

7. **Do robot vacuums with mopping function support both dry and wet mopping?**

- Yes, dry mopping removes floating dust and hair, while wet mopping removes stains. Switch between "Dry/Wet Mopping" modes in the APP; add clean water to the tank for wet mopping.

8. **Can models with automatic mop washing feature sweep only without washing the mop?**

- Yes, turn off the "Automatic Mop Washing" function and enable only suction mode. The robot will not perform mopping operations when the mop is not wet.

9. **Does the mop lift automatically after sweeping and mopping are completed?**

- Models supporting mop lifting will automatically raise the mop during recharging, crossing carpets, or after completing cleaning to prevent the mop from contaminating the floor. Enable this feature in the APP.

10. **How to set the number of cleaning passes for sweeping and mopping?**

- Select "Cleaning Frequency" in APP [Sweeping Settings], supporting adjustment from 1-3 times. Set multiple passes for stubborn stain areas.

### Dedicated Mopping System

11. **What to do if the water tank leaks after being filled?**

- Check whether the water tank lid is tightened, whether the water tank seal ring has fallen off or is damaged, confirm that the outlet pipe is not blocked, add water up to within the scale line, and avoid overfilling which causes overflow.

12. **How to adjust when the water output during mopping is too large or too small?**

- Enter [Mop Settings] in the APP; generally there are three levels of water output: low, medium, and high. Choose the low level for wooden floors, and the medium or high level for tiles and cement floors.

13. **Will the robot automatically stop when the water tank is empty?**

- Models supporting dry-tank detection will pause mopping after the water tank runs out of water, retaining only the sweeping function. Some models will push a dry-tank reminder to the APP, requiring manual refilling.

14. **How to add cleaning solution to the water tank?**

- It is recommended to use cleaning solution specifically designed for the model. Mix it with clean water at a ratio of 1:100 before adding. Do not add concentrated cleaning solution directly to avoid corroding the water tank and blocking the outlet pipe.

15. **What to do if the electric mop cloth does not rotate?**

- Check if the mop cloth is tangled with hair or debris, confirm if the mop motor buckle is installed in place. After cleaning, restart the robot. If it still does not rotate, contact after-sales service to inspect the motor.

16. **How to remove odor from the mop cloth after washing?**

- Soak the mop cloth in warm water + neutral detergent for 10 minutes then scrub, let it dry completely before use. Regularly disinfect the mop cloth at high temperature to prevent mold.

17. **How to install disposable mop cloths?**

- Lay the disposable mop cloth flat on the mop bracket, fasten the buckles around the four sides, ensure it fits tightly without looseness. Discard directly after use, no washing required.

18. **How often should washable mop cloths be cleaned?**

- They need to be washed after every cleaning session to avoid residue hardening. For households with pets or areas with oil stains, it is recommended to wash immediately after single use.

19. **How to solve striped water marks appearing on the floor during mopping?**

- Check if the mop cloth is dirty, hardened, or caked; clean or replace the mop cloth promptly. Reduce the water output, slow down the robot's cleaning speed to avoid leaving water marks due to fast mopping.

20. **What to do if corners and edges are not mopped cleanly?**

- Check the coverage range of the mop cloth. Some models support the "Corner Mopping Enhancement" function, which can be enabled in the APP. You can also manually assist in cleaning corners and edges, or use the spot mopping mode.

21. **What to do if the wastewater bin in the auto-mop-washing model is full?**

- When the APP pushes a wastewater bin full/overflow reminder, promptly dump the wastewater, clean the wastewater bin and filter mesh to avoid wastewater backflow contaminating the cleaning system.

22. **How to handle when the mop cloth is not washed despite adding water to the cleaning bin?**

- Check if the inlet pipe of the cleaning bin is blocked, confirm if the mop-washing pump is working normally. After cleaning, restart the robot. If the fault persists, contact after-sales service.

23. **How to fix a loose mop bracket?**

- Check if the bracket clips are broken. If the clips are intact, align the bracket with the body slot and press firmly until you hear a "click," then confirm there is no looseness before use.

24. **How to resolve the issue of the robot repeatedly staying in the same area during mopping?**

- Check if there are stubborn stains on the floor in that area; the robot will automatically identify them and focus cleaning there. If it is a misjudgment, you can set a "No-Mop Zone" in the APP or manually remote-control the robot away.

25. **How to maintain the water tank if it is not used for a long time?**

- Drain any remaining clean water from the tank, wipe the inside of the tank and the sealing ring with a dry cloth, and store it in a cool, dry place to prevent aging of the sealing ring.

### Cleaning Effect Optimization Category

26. **What to do if heavy oil stains on kitchen floors cannot be cleaned properly by mopping?**

- First enable high-suction mode to clear debris from the surface of the oil stains. Use dedicated cleaning solution with high water output and multiple mop-sweep passes. For stubborn oil stains, pre-treat them manually before letting the robot re-mop.

27. **How to clean pet urine stains?**

- First absorb most of the urine with paper towels, then dilute pet-specific deodorizing cleaning solution and add it to the water tank. Enable spot mopping mode, and after multiple cleanings, use dry mop mode to absorb moisture.

28. **How to handle stubborn stains such as gum or adhesive on the floor?**

- Do not use the robot to clean directly. Manually remove the top layer of adhesive with a scraper, wipe with cleaner, and then let the robot perform regular mopping and sweeping.

29. **How to set up so that carpet areas are not mopped?**

- Set a "No-Mop Zone" for carpet areas in the APP. When the robot sweeps into this area, it will automatically lift the mop pad or skip mopping, retaining only the sweeping function.

30. **How to solve the issue of the water tank running out of water mid-mop in large houses?**

- Choose a model that supports "resume mopping." After refilling, the robot will return to the location where it ran out of water to continue mopping. Alternatively, divide the map into zones and mop different areas in batches.

31. **What to do if wooden floors warp or deform after mopping?**

- Immediately absorb surface moisture from the wooden floor with a dry cloth and open windows and doors for ventilation. For subsequent mopping, switch to "dry mop" or the lowest water output setting to avoid standing water on the floor.

32. **How to handle dirt in tile grout lines that cannot be cleaned properly by mopping?**

- Enable the "high-frequency vibration mopping" feature (supported by some models), adjust water output to medium, use dedicated tile cleaning solution, and mop the grout line areas multiple times.

33. **What to do if fine dust remains on the floor after mopping and sweeping?**

- Check if the filter is clogged or if the main brush is worn, and clean or replace them promptly. Before mopping, enable "precision sweep" mode first, then mop, to avoid spreading dust with the mop pad.

34. **Can the robot mop outdoor balcony areas?**

- Not recommended. Outdoor areas have wind-blown sand and water stains, which can clog the robot's sensors and filters. Uneven ground may also cause robot malfunctions. It should only be used within enclosed balconies.

35. **How to clean snack stains and juice stains in a child's room?**

- Dilute a small amount of fruit and vegetable cleaner with water, add it to the water tank, enable spot mopping mode, and mop again with clean water after cleaning to avoid cleaner residue.

36. **How to make the robot avoid water stains at carpet edges while sweeping and mopping?**

- Set the carpet edge as a "buffer zone" in the APP; the robot will stop mopping 10cm from the carpet and only sweep, or you can enable the mop lifting function.

37. **How to solve residual floating dust on old concrete floors after sweeping and mopping?**

- First enable high suction mode and sweep repeatedly 2 times, then mop with medium water output, choose highly absorbent mop cloths, and promptly clean floating dust off the mop cloths after mopping.

38. **How to handle the robot easily colliding when mopping near mirror furniture?**

- Set a "collision prevention zone" at the bottom of mirror furniture, or wrap the bottom of the furniture with collision prevention strips to avoid collisions caused by reflection misjudgment without affecting mopping effectiveness.

39. **What to do if the robot pushes debris around instead of sucking it up during sweeping and mopping?**

- Check if the suction port is blocked, set suction to the highest level, clear tangles from the main brush and side brushes, and manually remove large debris before letting the robot work.

40. **How to optimize sweeping and mopping performance under beds and sofas?**

- Clear tangles under beds and sofas to ensure the robot can enter smoothly, set "deep sweep and mop" for this area in the APP, and increase suction power and mopping frequency.

### Hardware Failures and Alarms

41. **How to handle the "Mopping System Abnormal" alarm?**

- Check if the water tank and mop cloth are properly installed, if the mop motor is stuck, clear debris and hair from the mopping module, and contact after-sales service if the alarm persists after restarting the robot.

42. **What to do if the "Water Tank Not Detected" alarm appears but the tank is installed?**

- Check if the water tank sensor is obscured by dust or water stains, wipe the sensor with a dry soft cloth, reinstall the water tank, and ensure the buckle is securely fastened.

43. **How to resolve "clicking" noises when the mop rotates?**

- Remove the mop cloth, clear tangled hair, threads, and small debris inside the bracket, check if the mop motor bearings lack oil, and apply a small amount of dedicated lubricating oil (do not over-apply).

44. **How to handle the "Cleaning Tray Water Level Abnormal" alarm on auto-mop-washing models?**

- Check if clean water in the cleaning tray is filled to the mark, if dirty water is full, clear the water level sensor in the cleaning tray, refill water and empty dirty water, then restart.

45. **What to do if the robot suddenly stops while mopping?**

- Check remaining battery; low battery prioritizes returning to charge. If battery is sufficient, check if the mopping module is stuck or sensors are obscured, clear them, and retry.

46. **How to resolve the "Mop Bracket Not Installed" alarm?**

- Check whether the mop bracket is fully locked into the body slot and whether the bracket clips are damaged. If damaged, replace with a new bracket, reinstall it, and restart the robot.

47. **How to clear a clogged water tank outlet pipe?**

- Gently clear the outlet pipe opening using a toothpick or thin wire, avoiding excessive force that may damage the pipe; then rinse the inside of the water tank with clean water to ensure smooth water flow.

48. **How to handle robot wobbling or drifting during mopping?**

- Check if the mop is installed flatly, if the drive wheels are jammed by debris, and if there is standing water on the floor causing slipping. After cleaning, place the robot on a flat surface and restart it.

49. **What to do if the automatic mop lifting function fails?**

- Confirm in the APP that this function is enabled, check if the mop lifting motor is stuck, clear debris around the motor, and contact after-sales service if it still fails after restarting the robot.

50. **How to handle the "Cleaning solution concentration too high" alarm?**

- Empty all liquid from the water tank, rinse the tank multiple times with clean water, remix the cleaning solution and water according to the ratio, refill, and restart the robot.

51. **How to resolve black scratches left by the robot during mopping?**

- Check if the mop bracket has wear or peeling paint, and if sand particles or hard objects are trapped in the mop. Clean the mop promptly and replace worn bracket accessories.

52. **How to handle the situation where the mopping module continues to work while the robot is charging?**

- Restart the robot to confirm if the issue is caused by system lag leading to functional abnormalities. If the fault persists, restore factory settings in the APP and reconfigure cleaning parameters.

53. **How to resolve the "Dirty water tank filter clogged" alarm?**

- Remove the dirty water tank filter, rinse off debris and stains from the filter surface with clean water, let it dry completely before reinstalling, and clean the filter regularly to prevent future clogging.

54. **How to handle when the mop motor does not rotate and the body heats up?**

- Immediately turn off the robot's power to avoid burning out the motor. Check if the motor is jammed by debris. If you cannot clean it yourself, contact after-sales service for inspection and repair.

55. **How to resolve the issue where the mopping function automatically turns off after the robot crosses a threshold?**

- Check if the threshold height exceeds the robot's compatible range. Enable the "Resume mopping after crossing obstacles" function (supported by some models), manually assist the robot across the threshold, and then restart mopping.

### Consumables and Maintenance

56. **What is the lifespan of the mop?**

- Washable mops should be replaced every 3-6 months under normal use. For households with pets or areas with heavy oil stains, replace them every 1-2 months. Disposable mops are for single use and should be replaced immediately after use.

57. **How often should the water tank sealing ring be replaced?**

- The sealing ring is a consumable part. It is recommended to replace it every 6-12 months. Replace it immediately if aging, cracking, or leaking occurs.

58. **How often should the cleaning brush of an automatic mop-washing model be cleaned?**

- Clean hair and debris from the cleaning brush every time you empty the dirty water, rinse it with clean water once a week, and replace it promptly if wear or deformation occurs.

59. **Do the bearings of the mopping module require maintenance?**

- Yes, apply 1-2 drops of specialized lubricating oil to the bearings every 3 months to prevent rusting and sticking, ensuring smooth rotation of the mop cloth.

60. **Can filters be used in the mopping system?**

- No, the filter specifications and functions for sweeping and mopping systems are different. Mopping filters mainly filter dirty water and require dedicated filters; mixing them can damage the device.

61. **How do you clean the sensors of the mopping system?**

- Gently wipe the sensor surface with a dry soft cloth or lint-free cloth. Avoid using wet cloths or alcohol to prevent moisture damage or malfunction.

62. **How should consumables be replaced in models with auto-empty + auto-mop-washing features?**

- Replace dust bags every 1-2 months, replace mop cloths every 3 months, clean filters every 1-2 months and replace them every 6 months. Adjust flexibly based on usage frequency.

63. **If the clip on the mop holder breaks, can it be repaired?**

- Broken clips cannot be repaired; you must replace the holder with the same original equipment manufacturer (OEM) part. Using non-OEM accessories may result in poor mopping performance or body damage.

64. **How do you maintain the water pump of an automatic mop-washing model?**

- When not in use for a long period, drain water from the cleaning tank and water tank, rinse the pump pipes with clean water to prevent scale and dirt blockage, and clean with descaler every 3 months.

65. **Can disposable mop cloths be washed and reused?**

- Not recommended. Disposable mop cloths are designed for single-use absorbency and cleaning effectiveness. Washing makes them hard and causes shedding, affecting mopping results and potentially clogging the mopping module.

### Scenario Adaptation and Usage Tips

66. **How to use the robot for mopping during humid weather in southern regions?**

- Switch to "dry mop" mode, turn off water output, and use the mop's absorbency to clear ground humidity. Simultaneously turn on air conditioning dehumidification to avoid re-humidifying the floor after mopping.

67. **What should be noted when mopping in northern winter homes with underfloor heating?**

- Set to low water output, open windows for ventilation immediately after mopping/sweeping to avoid rapid evaporation of standing water caused by underfloor heating, which leads to water stains. Choose thin, highly absorbent mop cloths.

68. **For renters without balconies, how to dry mop cloths?**

- After washing, absorb moisture with a dry cloth, place in a well-ventilated indoor area, or dry with a hair dryer on cold setting. Avoid storing in enclosed, humid environments.

69. **What should be noted when mopping in households with infants?**

- Use only clean water for mopping to avoid residue from adding cleaning solutions; after mopping, wipe the floor again with a dry cloth to ensure there is no standing water or detergent residue on the floor, and allow the floor to dry completely before letting infants play.

70. **How to use a robot vacuum and mop combo on multi-level floors?**

- Create separate maps for each floor, set dedicated vacuuming and mopping parameters, manually move the robot to the corresponding floor before starting cleaning, and avoid automatic cross-floor cleaning which may cause failures in the mopping system.

71. **If there is a washing machine on the balcony, how to prevent the robot from contacting standing water while mopping?**

- Set a "No-Mop Zone" around the washing machine, clear standing water and stains from the balcony floor, and ensure the ground in the robot's working area is dry.

72. **With severe pet shedding, how to prevent hair from tangling in the mop pad?**

- First enable high-suction mode to thoroughly remove hair, then proceed with mopping; choose an anti-tangle mop pad style, and promptly clean hair off the mop pad after each mopping session.

73. **During long business trips, how to set up automatic vacuuming and mopping for the robot?**

- Set "Scheduled Vacuuming and Mopping" in the APP, select appropriate water output and suction levels, ensure the water tank is full and the dust bin is empty, and enable the "Auto Return to Charge After Completion" function.

74. **Can a robot vacuum and mop combo be used for post-renovation initial cleaning?**

- Not recommended. After renovation, the floor contains large amounts of cement debris, sand particles, and sharp objects, which can scratch the robot body and the floor, clog filters, and jam mechanical parts. Manual initial cleaning is required before using the robot.

75. **How to quickly clean snack crumbs that fall from tables or windowsills onto the floor?**

- Enable "Spot Cleaning Mode", position the robot at the spill area, set high suction + medium water output, and quickly clean up snack crumbs and stains.

76. **How to make the robot avoid light small obstacles such as slippers and toys?**

- Enable "Advanced Obstacle Avoidance", store light small obstacles properly, or set "Virtual Walls" around obstacles to prevent abnormal mopping functions caused by collisions.

77. **There is a non-slip mat at the bathroom entrance. How should the robot handle it while mopping?**

- Set a "No-Mop Zone" at the non-slip mat location. The robot will only vacuum the mat without mopping, to prevent mold and bacterial growth caused by the mat absorbing water.

78. **In summer, how to clean watermelon juice and ice cream stains on the floor?**

- Immediately clean using spot mopping mode, add clean water with a small amount of fruit and vegetable cleaner, and use dry mop mode to absorb moisture after mopping, to prevent stains from drying out and becoming difficult to clean.

79. **The study has carpets and desks. How to perform precise vacuuming and mopping?**

- Set a "No-Mop Zone" for the carpets, set "Spot Cleaning" around the desks, reduce water output, and avoid water stains contaminating books and carpets.

80. **The residential area has heavy dust. How to reduce the frequency of robot vacuuming and mopping?**

- Close doors and windows to reduce dust entry, set a combination mode of "High Suction + Medium Water Output" in the APP, achieve deep cleaning with one pass of vacuuming and mopping, and appropriately lower the cleaning frequency.

### Software and Function Settings

81. **How to save and delete the mopping and sweeping map in the APP?**

- After mapping is complete, the robot automatically saves the map. Multiple maps can be manually saved in the APP's [Map Management]. To delete a map, select the corresponding map and click "Delete"; after deletion, you can re-map.

82. **How to share sweeping and mopping settings with family members?**

- In the APP, go to [Device Sharing], invite family members via phone number or QR code. Family members can view and use the configured sweeping and mopping parameters, and also make minor adjustments themselves.

83. **How to view and delete the robot's sweeping and mopping records?**

- In the APP's [Cleaning Records], you can view the area, duration, zones, and cleaning effectiveness of each sweep-and-mop session. Long-press a single record to delete it, or select "Batch Delete".

84. **How to set scheduled times for sweeping and mopping?**

- Add a scheduled task in the APP's [Scheduled Cleaning], selecting the sweep-and-mop mode, water output, suction power, and cleaning zones. Set the specific time and repetition frequency; after saving, the robot will work on schedule.

85. **How to turn off mopping and sweeping reminders pushed by the APP?**

- In the APP's [Settings] - [Message Notifications], turn off unnecessary push notifications such as "Low Water Reminder", "Consumable Replacement Reminder", and "Cleaning Completion Reminder".

86. **Will sweeping and mopping settings be lost after the robot system upgrade?**

- No, system upgrades retain original settings. If setting anomalies occur, you can restore factory settings in the APP and reconfigure them.

87. **How to customize the sweeping and mopping route?**

- For models supporting "Custom Route", draw the sweep-and-mop path in the APP. The robot will clean according to the drawn route, suitable for precise sweeping and mopping of key areas.

88. **Can the robot perform sweeping and mopping tasks normally when the phone is offline?**

- Yes, as long as the robot is connected to WiFi and has scheduled sweep-and-mop tasks set up, it will continue to work according to preset parameters even if the phone is offline. You can view cleaning records in the APP once online.

89. **How to restore sweeping and mopping modes to default settings?**

- Click "Restore Defaults" in the APP's [Cleaning Settings]. All parameters such as suction power, water output, and cleaning passes will revert to factory settings, allowing for new customization.

90. **How to check the remaining lifespan of mopping consumables in the APP?**

- In the APP's [Consumable Management], you can view the usage time and remaining lifespan of consumables like mop pads, sealing rings, and filters. A replacement reminder will be pushed when consumables are nearing expiration.

### After-sales and Upgrades

91. **Does the warranty for the all-in-one sweeping and mopping robot cover the mopping module?**

- Yes, within the whole-machine warranty scope (generally 1-2 years), non-consumable parts of the mopping module such as the motor, water tank, and mop bracket are covered for free repair or replacement if they fail. Consumables (mop pads, sealing rings) are not covered under warranty.

92. **Is the repair cost high for mopping system failures after the warranty expires?**

- Repair costs depend on the faulty component; core parts such as the mop motor and water pump are more expensive, while small accessories like filters and clips are cheaper. It is recommended to choose official after-sales repair to ensure original parts.

93. **Can old all-in-one sweeping and mopping robots be upgraded with mop lifting functionality?**

- No, mop lifting functionality requires hardware and system support. Older models lack the corresponding hardware and cannot achieve this through system upgrades. You can replace them with new models that support this feature.

94. **Do sweeping and mopping settings need to be reset after replacing the robot's motherboard?**

- Yes, after replacing the motherboard, the robot's system and settings will be cleared. You need to reconnect WiFi, rebuild the map, set sweeping and mopping parameters, and configure scheduled tasks.

95. **How to check the model number of original accessories for an all-in-one sweeping and mopping robot?**

- Check the product manual, the model label on the bottom of the body, or enter the robot model in the brand's official flagship store or APP mall to match the corresponding original accessories.

96. **Can third-party mops be used on original all-in-one sweeping and mopping robots?**

- It is recommended to use original mops. Third-party mops may have dimensions or absorbency that do not match the model, which can affect mopping performance and even wear out the mopping module.

97. **After using the robot for 1 year, if mopping performance deteriorates, what accessories need to be upgraded?**

- Prioritize replacing the mop, filter, and main brush. Clean debris and limescale from the mopping module. If performance is still poor, check the motor suction power and mop rotation frequency, and replace motor components if necessary.

98. **What inspections are needed when reusing a sweeping and mopping robot after moving?**

- Check the body and mopping module for collision damage. Clean the dust bin, filter, mop, and sensors. Rebuild the map and calibrate sweeping and mopping parameters. Test whether all functions work normally.

99. **Can an all-in-one sweeping and mopping robot be sent back to the manufacturer for deep maintenance?**

- Yes, brand officials generally support send-back deep maintenance services, including whole-machine cleaning, consumable replacement, and function calibration. Specific costs and procedures can be consulted with official customer service.

100. **How to determine if an all-in-one sweeping and mopping robot needs to be replaced with a new one?**

- If the robot has been used for more than 3 years and experiences severe suction decline, frequent mopping module failures, or chaotic mapping with high repair costs (exceeding 1/3 of the price of a new machine), it is recommended to replace it with a new one. New models offer better sweeping and mopping functions and obstacle avoidance.
