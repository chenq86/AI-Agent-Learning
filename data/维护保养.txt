# Robot Vacuum + Robot Vacuum & Mop Combo Maintenance Guide: 200 Tips (Pure Maintenance Perspective, Divided into General Basics / Vacuum-Only Specifics / Mopping-Specifics for Combo Units / Consumables Special / Environmental Adaptation / Long-Term Storage / Fault Prevention, Covering Daily Maintenance for All Models)

## General Basic Maintenance (50 Items)

1. After daily use, wipe the robot's body shell with a dry soft cloth to remove dust and water stains, preventing dirt from adhering and hardening.

2. After each cleaning session, promptly clear hair and threads from the gaps in the body's bumper strip to prevent jamming that affects rebound.

3. Check the robot's bottom drive wheels and caster wheel daily; use tweezers to pick out tangled hair and debris to ensure smooth wheel rotation.

4. During daily use, avoid colliding the robot with sharp hard objects (such as table corners or metal racks) to prevent damage to the body shell and sensors.

5. Clean the robot's charging contacts daily by wiping the metal contacts on both the body and the charging dock with a dry cloth to avoid poor contact due to oxidation.

6. When the robot is operating, avoid touching the rotating main brush and side brushes with your hands to prevent pinching injuries and accessory damage.

7. Check the robot's bottom anti-drop and edge-following sensors daily; gently wipe their surfaces with a dry cloth to ensure they are free of dust and water stain obstruction.

8. When cleaning the robot, disconnect the power supply throughout the process. Never rinse the body directly with water (waterproof models may only be wiped, not soaked).

9. Clear debris from the dust bin clips daily to ensure the dust bin is installed properly, avoiding dust leakage and alarms.

10. After each use, return the robot to the charging dock to ensure it can recharge at any time, avoiding storage in a discharged state.

11. Check the screws on the robot's body weekly; tighten them immediately if loose to prevent accessories from falling off.

12. Use a soft-bristle brush to clean the robot's heat dissipation vents weekly to avoid motor overheating protection triggered by dust blockage.

13. Press the bumper strip lightly multiple times weekly to check if the rebound is normal; if it jams, disassemble and clean internal debris promptly.

14. Wipe the robot's APP connection indicator light and buttons weekly to avoid dust coverage affecting operation and display.

15. Apply 1-2 drops of dedicated lubricant to the axles of the caster wheel and drive wheels monthly to prevent rust and abnormal noises (do not apply too much to avoid attracting dust).

16. Check the robot's battery compartment monthly to confirm the battery has no swelling or leakage, and that the cable connections are secure without looseness.

17. Wipe the robot's obstacle avoidance camera/sensors with a dry cloth monthly to avoid dust and fingerprints affecting recognition accuracy.

18. Perform a comprehensive dust removal on the robot's body once a month, including dead corners such as the bottom, back, and charging port.

19. If the robot produces slight abnormal noises, stop it immediately for inspection; do not force usage to avoid expanding the fault.

20. Before replacing any accessories, always disconnect the power and confirm that the accessory model matches the device model to avoid mixing incompatible parts.

21. Avoid letting the robot operate for extended periods in high temperatures (>40°C) or low temperatures (<0°C) to prevent damage to the battery and motor.

22. Avoid exposing the robot to corrosive liquids (such as alcohol, disinfectants, or dish soap) to prevent aging of the body and accessories.

23. After the robot completes its work, if the body is stained with oil, dilute a neutral detergent and gently wipe it off, then dry it with a clean cloth.

24. Check daily that the charging dock is placed on a flat surface with no obstructions around it to ensure smooth return-to-charge for the robot.

25. Clean the infrared sensor area of the charging dock weekly by wiping it with a dry cloth to prevent dust from affecting the reception of the return-to-charge signal.

26. Avoid squeezing or tangling the power cord of the charging dock; check weekly that the plug and cable are undamaged to prevent short circuits.

27. Do not let the robot operate or be stored in direct sunlight for extended periods to prevent fading of the casing and abnormal light sensing by the sensors.

28. Check the clips of the main brush and side brushes monthly to confirm there are no breaks or looseness, ensuring accessories are securely installed.

29. When moving the robot during daily use, handle it gently; never drag or drop it to prevent internal components from shifting.

30. Check the dust bin air outlet of the robot weekly to ensure it is not blocked, maintaining normal suction power.

31. If the robot encounters large particle debris (such as stones or bottle caps) while working, manually remove them promptly to avoid jamming mechanical parts.

32. Use a soft-bristle brush monthly to clear debris from the robot's suction inlet to prevent blockage that affects vacuuming performance.

33. Check daily for obvious scratches or deformation on the robot; if deformation occurs due to collision, contact after-sales service for correction promptly.

34. Avoid applying stickers with strong adhesives to the robot's body, as they may leave adhesive residue upon removal and corrode the casing.

35. Wipe the robot's dust collection port (for models with auto-empty stations) weekly to ensure a tight seal and prevent dust leakage.

36. Avoid using the robot on floors with significant standing water to prevent water ingress into the body causing short circuits.

37. Check the interface of the mopping module (for sweep-and-mop combo models) monthly to ensure it is free of dust and debris, allowing for smooth connection.

38. After daily use, set the robot's cleaning mode to "Standby" to reduce battery consumption.

39. Check the power adapter of the charging dock weekly for overheating or unusual noises, avoiding prolonged operation under overload conditions.

40. If the robot accidentally comes into contact with pet feces, immediately clean it thoroughly with wet wipes, then wipe with a neutral detergent to prevent bacterial growth.

41. Check the rotation of the robot's side brushes daily; if they stutter, stop the machine immediately and clean them to prevent motor burnout.

42. Avoid letting the robot operate in areas with high drops (such as stairways); even with anti-drop features, proper protection should be ensured.

43. Perform an "idle run test" on the robot monthly: start it without load and run for 1 minute to check for unusual noises or stuttering.

44. Wipe silicone accessories on the robot's body (such as seals and bumper strips) weekly with a dry cloth to prevent aging and cracking.

45. Clear debris from the entrance of the roller brush chamber daily to prevent items from entering and tangling around the main brush.

46. Avoid using stiff-bristled brushes or steel wool to clean robot accessories to prevent scratching surfaces and damaging sensors.

47. Check the robot's APP connection status weekly to ensure normal network operation and timely receipt of consumable and fault alerts.

48. When the robot is charging, ensure the charging environment is well-ventilated and free from flammable or explosive items.

49. Inspect the robot's anti-tangle comb teeth (if present) monthly to ensure they are not deformed or broken and can properly comb hair.

50. Follow the principle of "gentle use, gentle placement, and prompt cleaning" in daily use to reduce accessory wear and extend the overall lifespan of the unit.

## Dedicated Maintenance for Sweeping Function (30 Items)

1. Immediately after each cleaning session, remove hair and threads from the main brush by cutting tangled objects with scissors; do not pull them forcefully.

2. Clean dust and debris from the side brushes daily to ensure they can normally sweep corner dust into the suction port during rotation.

3. Disassemble the main brush roller housing weekly and use a soft-bristle brush to clean dust and hair inside the housing, leaving no blind spots.

4. If the main brush "slips" during use, check if the roller brush rubber strip is worn and clean or replace it promptly.

5. Clean the robot's air duct weekly using a soft-bristle brush, sweeping from the dust bin outlet towards the suction port to ensure the duct remains clear.

6. For robots with auto-empty stations, inspect the dust collection channel daily to ensure it is free from blockages caused by hair or debris, guaranteeing smooth dust collection.

7. After cleaning the dust bin each time, wipe the interior with a dry cloth to prevent dust clumping; do not soak it in water (except for waterproof dust bins).

8. The dust bin filter must be installed together with the dust bin and cannot be missing, to prevent dust from entering the motor and causing damage.

9. If suction power decreases during sweeping, first check whether the dust bin, filter, and air duct are blocked; clean them one by one and retry.

10. Weekly inspection of the robot's suction port roller brush (for models without a main brush) to ensure there is no tangling or wear and that it rotates smoothly.

11. For robots with auto-empty stations, check daily that the dust bag is properly installed and tightly sealed to avoid dust leakage.

12. After the robot cleans on long-pile carpets, immediately remove carpet fibers from the main brush to prevent tangling and hardening.

13. Monthly inspection of the main brush motor's working condition; if abnormal heating or noise occurs during operation, stop the machine immediately.

14. When cleaning the main brush, take care to protect the bearings at both ends of the roller brush to avoid jamming due to water or dust ingress.

15. Replace the side brush immediately when the bristle length wears down to <1cm to avoid affecting corner cleaning performance.

16. Use a soft-bristle brush weekly to clean the robot's dust sensor to prevent misjudgment of environmental dust levels leading to abnormal suction.

17. For robots with auto-empty stations, clean the dust station's air outlet weekly to ensure it is unobstructed and maintains normal suction power for dust collection.

18. After the robot cleans on rough surfaces such as concrete floors or tiles, promptly clean sand and mud from the bottom of the chassis to prevent wear on the wheel assembly.

19. After each cleaning session, completely empty the trash from the dust bin to avoid bacterial growth and odor generation from prolonged storage.

20. Check the robot's suction adjustment module monthly to ensure that suction switching between all levels is normal and free of jamming.

21. When cleaning hair, if the main brush has an anti-tangle design, use the comb teeth to quickly strip away hair, improving cleaning efficiency.

22. If the robot supports "carpet boost," check after use whether the boost module resets properly, with no jamming or abnormal noises.

23. Check the side brush clips weekly to ensure the side brushes are securely installed and do not loosen or shift during rotation.

24. If the dust bin has an odor, place an activated carbon pack inside to absorb it, or gently wipe with a neutral deodorizer, then let it dry before use.

25. After using the robot in environments with pets, immediately clean the main brush and dust bin to prevent pet hair from clumping.

26. Disassemble the robot's suction inlet assembly monthly to clean out small internal debris, ensuring the suction inlet remains clear.

27. For robots with auto-empty stations, turn off the power before replacing the dust bag to avoid dust leakage during the emptying process.

28. If the main roller brush is detachable, remove and wash it once a week (wipe with a dry cloth for non-waterproof models), then install it after drying.

29. After the robot cleans large-particle trash, immediately check the suction inlet and roller brush compartment for residues to prevent machine jams.

30. Check the robot's motor cooling fan monthly to ensure there is no dust blockage, guaranteeing proper heat dissipation for the motor.

## Exclusive Maintenance for Sweeping and Mopping Integrated Floor Cleaning Functions (40 items)

1. Immediately after each mopping session, remove the mop pad, rinse it thoroughly with clean water, wring it out, and place it in a well-ventilated area to dry, preventing mold growth.

2. Clean the mop holder daily by using a soft-bristle brush to pick out tangled hair and threads, ensuring the holder is clean and free of debris.

3. Before each use of the water tank, check that there are no impurities or scale inside; add clean water when refilling to avoid generating scale from direct tap water addition.

4. After mopping, drain any remaining clean water from the tank, wipe the interior and outlet with a dry cloth to prevent clogging due to scale.

5. Soak the water tank in citric acid solution (diluted 1:50) for 10 minutes weekly to remove scale, then rinse thoroughly with clean water and let it dry.

6. For models with automatic mop washing, empty the dirty water tank after each use, rinse the dirty water tank and filter with clean water, leaving no stain residue.

7. For models with automatic mop washing, clean the scrubbing brush in the cleaning station daily by picking out hair and debris to ensure effective mop washing.

8. Apply 1 drop of specialized lubricant to the pivot point of the mop holder monthly to prevent rusting and jamming, ensuring smooth mop rotation.

9. Before installing the mop pad each time, check that it is clean and not hardened to ensure no secondary contamination occurs during mopping.

10. Wipe the water tank seal ring with a dry cloth weekly, checking for aging, cracking, or deformation to ensure the tank seals tightly without leaking.

11. For models with automatic mop washing, clean the water level sensor in the cleaning station weekly by gently wiping with a dry cloth to avoid false readings caused by stain obstruction.

12. If water output is abnormal during mopping, immediately clean the water tank outlet and pipe, using a toothpick to gently unclog them to prevent blockages.

13. For electric mop models, inspect the mop motor after each use for abnormal heating or noise, and promptly clear any debris around the motor.

14. If the mop has an odor, soak it in warm water + neutral detergent for 15 minutes, scrub it clean, scald it with boiling water once, and let it dry before use.

15. Before installing the water tank each time, check the body's water tank interface to ensure there is no dust or debris, guaranteeing smooth connection without leakage.

16. For models with automatic mop lifting, inspect the lifting module daily to ensure there is no jamming or misalignment, and that the mop lifts and lowers normally.

17. Discard disposable mops immediately after use; do not reuse them to avoid shedding fibers or clogging the mopping module.

18. Wipe the bottom of the mopping module weekly to remove water stains and dirt, preventing bacterial growth and odors.

19. For auto-washing mop models, use descaling agent monthly to clean the pump pipes in the cleaning station, avoiding limescale blockages that prevent water output.

20. When adding cleaning solution to the water tank, dilute strictly according to the ratio; do not add concentrated cleaning solution directly to prevent corrosion of the tank and pipes.

21. After mopping, wipe the mopping module interface on the robot's bottom with a dry cloth to ensure no water stains or dust residue remain.

22. Check the mop's adhesive/clip design weekly to ensure there is no looseness or detachment, keeping the mop securely installed.

23. For auto-washing mop models, clean the drain outlet of the cleaning station after each use to ensure no debris blockage and smooth drainage.

24. If the mop sheds fibers during use, replace it immediately to avoid lint remaining on the floor and affecting cleaning performance.

25. Inspect the water tank's outlet valve monthly to ensure normal opening and closing, no jamming or leaking, and smooth water flow.

26. Avoid exposing plastic components of the mopping module to high temperatures or corrosive liquids to prevent aging and deformation.

27. For auto-washing mop models, inspect the filter mesh of the dirty water tank weekly to ensure no blockage or damage, maintaining filtration effectiveness and preventing backflow of dirty water.

28. Before each mopping session, gently pat the mop to remove loose dust, then install and use it to avoid contaminating the floor with loose dust.

29. If the water tank is unused for a long period, wipe the sealing ring with a dry cloth and apply a thin layer of silicone oil to the sealing ring to prevent aging.

30. For electric mop models, inspect the mop's rotation speed monthly to ensure there is no slowdown or jamming; if present, clean or repair promptly.

31. If scratches appear on the floor during mopping, immediately check if sand particles or hard objects are trapped inside the mop, and clean them out before further use.

32. For auto-washing mop models, inspect the fresh water filling port of the cleaning station daily to ensure no dust or debris blockage and smooth filling.

33. Clean stains and limescale from the mop bracket weekly using a soft-bristle brush lightly, to avoid difficulty in cleaning once they harden.

34. After mopping, remove the mop bracket and place it in a ventilated area to dry, avoiding bacterial growth caused by contact with the robot body.

35. Inspect the wiring harness of the mopping module monthly to ensure no damage or looseness, preventing short circuits or poor contact.

36. If debris accidentally enters the water tank, immediately empty the fresh water, use tweezers to remove the debris, and then rinse thoroughly with clean water.

37. For models with automatic mop washing, wipe the exterior of the cleaning station with a dry cloth after use to remove water stains and dirt, keeping it clean.

38. After using mops for 3-5 times, disinfect them once with high-temperature steam to kill bacteria and mites, especially suitable for households with pets/infants.

39. Check the motor of auto-lift mop models monthly; ensure there is no abnormal heating during operation. If detected, contact after-sales service for inspection promptly.

40. All accessories of the mopping module must be air-dried before reinstallation after disassembly and cleaning to avoid damage to motors and circuits caused by water ingress.

## Specialized Maintenance and Replacement of Consumables (30 items)

1. HEPA filter: Gently brush surface dust with a soft-bristle brush weekly. Rinse with clean water every 1-2 months (do not rub). Dry in a cool, shaded place. Replace with a new filter every 3-6 months.

2. Main brush: Replace every 3-6 months for ordinary households. Replace every 1-3 months for households with pets or long-pile carpet environments. Replace immediately if wear, deformation, or cracking occurs.

3. Side brushes: Replace when bristles wear down to <1cm, deform, or break. Generally replace every 2-3 months. Replace every 1 month in dusty environments.

4. Dustbin filter (pre-filter): Clean surface dust daily. Rinse with clean water monthly. Use after drying. Replace every 6 months.

5. Mops (washable): Replace every 3-6 months under normal use. Replace every 1-2 months in environments with pets/heavy oil stains. Replace immediately if hardening, shedding, or damage occurs.

6. Water tank seal ring: Replace every 6-12 months. If aging, cracking, or leaking occurs, replace immediately without waiting.

7. Dust bags (for models with dust collection): Replace every 1-2 months for ordinary households. Replace every half-month to 1 month for dusty/pet households. Replace promptly when dust capacity reaches 80%.

8. Cleaning brush (for automatic mop washing models): Remove hair weekly. Rinse with clean water monthly. Replace every 3-6 months. Replace immediately if wear or deformation occurs.

9. Caster wheels/drive wheels: Replace when wheel surfaces are severely worn, rotation is stuck, or abnormal noises occur. Generally replace every 1-2 years. Replace every 6-12 months on rough floors.

10. Roller brush rubber strip (main brush accessory): Replace when wear, cracking, or detachment occurs. Generally replaced synchronously with the main brush.

11. Dirty water tank filter (sweep-and-mop integrated): Rinse with clean water weekly. Use after drying. Replace every 6 months. Replace immediately if damaged.

12. Battery: Replace after 2-3 years of normal use. Replace with an original battery immediately if runtime decays to below 50% of the original, fails to charge, or bulges.

13. Disposable mops: Single-use only. Replace as needed. Do not reuse.

14. Anti-tangle comb teeth: Replace when deformation or breakage occurs. Generally check every 6-12 months. Check every 3 months in households with pets.

15. Charging contact copper plates: If oxidation or rusting occurs, gently wipe with a rust remover. Replace the contact parts if they cannot be repaired.

16. Mopping module cleaning brush (sweep-and-mop integrated): Replace synchronously with the mop. Replace immediately if wear or shedding occurs.

17. Air duct filter: Clean every 3-6 months. Replace every 1-2 years. Replace earlier if severely clogged.

18. Side brush clips: Replace with the same model of original clips immediately if they break, loosen, or fail to secure the side brush.

19. Mop holder: Replace with an original holder if clips break, deform, or if the pivot is stuck and cannot be repaired.

20. Dust collection base filter (dust collection model): Clean monthly, replace every 6 months; replace early if damaged or severely clogged.

21. Suction port roller brush (models without main brush): Same replacement cycle as the main brush, replace every 3-6 months; for households with pets, replace every 1-3 months.

22. Water tank outlet valve: Replace with an original outlet valve accessory when leaking occurs or opening/closing is stuck and cannot be repaired.

23. Anti-collision strip: Replace with an original anti-collision strip when cracking, detachment, or abnormal rebound occurs; do not substitute with ordinary rubber strips.

24. Cleaning solution: Use within 3 months after opening; store sealed in a cool, dry place, avoiding direct sunlight.

25. Lubricating oil: Use lubricating oil specifically designed for robots; do not substitute with engine oil, grease, etc., to prevent dust adhesion and component damage.

26. Mop pad hook-and-loop fastener: Replace the hook-and-loop fastener accessory when its adhesive strength decreases and it can no longer secure the mop pad, or directly replace the mop pad bracket.

27. Robot foot pads: Replace foot pads when severe wear causes body wobbling, ensuring stable operation of the robot body.

28. Power cord (charging dock): Immediately replace with an original power cord when damage, aging, or overheating occurs; do not substitute with non-original cords.

29. Mop washing pump filter (sweeping and mopping integrated models): Clean weekly, replace every 6 months; replace early if clogging prevents water output.

30. For all consumables, prioritize **original accessories**; non-original accessories may have mismatched dimensions and specifications, easily leading to device malfunctions and reduced cleaning performance.

## Environmental Adaptation Maintenance (20 items)

1. Wooden floor environment: Set water output to low level during mopping, use dry mop mode to avoid standing water on floors; clean robot wheel assemblies weekly to prevent sand and mud from wearing down the flooring.

2. Tile/marble environment: Promptly clean water stains from the bottom of the robot after mopping; descale mop pad brackets monthly to prevent scale buildup.

3. Long-pile carpet environment: Immediately clean carpet fibers from the main brush and side brushes after sweeping; disable mopping function to prevent mop pads from wetting the carpet and causing mold.

4. Short-pile carpet environment: After using carpet boost function, check if the main brush has reset; clean dust along carpet edges weekly to prevent missed sweeps by the robot.

5. Concrete/old tile floor environment: Immediately clean sand and mud from the robot's bottom and wheel assemblies after sweeping to prevent component wear; reduce mopping frequency to avoid dust clumping.

6. Households with pets: Thoroughly clean the main brush, dust bin, and mop pads after each sweep; deodorize and disinfect the robot weekly; shorten consumable replacement cycles by 50%.

7. Households with infants/children: Use only clean water for mopping, avoid adding cleaning solutions; high-temperature disinfect mop pads after each use; wipe the robot body daily.

8. High-dust/sandstorm-prone environments: Clean the dust bin and filters daily; replace side brushes weekly; disable mopping function to prevent dust and water stains from mixing and clumping.

9. Southern humid 'return-of-south' weather/damp environments: Reduce mopping frequency, use dry mop mode; air-dry the robot body and consumables daily; perform dehumidification treatment on the robot weekly.

10. Northern dry/high-dust environments: Increase suction power level; clean the dust bin daily; deep-clean air ducts and filters monthly; set water output to medium level during mopping.

11. Kitchen heavy-oil-grease environments: Immediately clean mop pads and water tank after mopping; wash mop pads with neutral detergent; descale the water tank weekly to prevent oil/grease clogging.

12. Bathroom environment: Only perform sweeping, do not mop, to prevent the robot from contacting standing water; wipe the robot's bottom after cleaning to ensure no water stains remain.

13. Enclosed balcony environment: Open windows for ventilation promptly after mopping, clean sand and mud from the robot's wheel assembly, and avoid mixing balcony dust with water stains.

14. Duplex/multi-story floors: Create separate maps for each floor; before switching floors, clean the robot's bottom and sensors, and calibrate sweeping and mopping parameters.

15. Environments with mirrored/glass furniture: Clean the robot's obstacle avoidance sensors before sweeping to prevent mapping errors caused by reflections; wipe the sensors weekly.

16. Low-clearance environments under furniture: After the robot enters, manually assist in cleaning dead corners; clean the robot's bottom sensors weekly to prevent dust obstruction.

17. Environments with thresholds/steps: After the robot crosses them, check if the wheel assembly or mopping module is stuck; manually assist in cleaning dust at the thresholds.

18. High-temperature summer environments: Avoid letting the robot work in direct sunlight; ensure good ventilation in the charging environment; check battery temperature weekly to prevent overheating.

19. Low-temperature winter environments: Let the robot sit in a room-temperature environment for 30 minutes before operation to avoid battery life degradation; ensure the ambient temperature is ≥5°C during charging.

20. Post-renovation cleaning environments: Perform initial manual cleaning first; remove cement residue, sand grains, and sharp debris before using the robot to avoid jamming mechanical parts or scratching accessories.

## Long-term Storage Maintenance (20 items)

1. Before storage, fully charge the robot (to 80%-90% capacity) to avoid battery damage caused by storing it in a discharged state.

2. Before storage, thoroughly clean all accessories including the robot body, dust bin, filter, main brush, side brushes, and mop cloth, ensuring no residual dust or stains.

3. For integrated sweeping and mopping robots, drain all water from the water tank and dirty water tank before storage; wipe them dry with a dry cloth and let them air dry before storing.

4. Remove all detachable accessories (such as mop cloths, mop holders, dust bags, filters), wash and dry them, then store them separately to avoid bacterial growth caused by contact with the body.

5. Before storage, thoroughly wipe the robot's battery compartment, charging contacts, and sensors to ensure they are free of dust and water stains.

6. When storing, place the robot in a **cool, dry, and well-ventilated** environment, avoiding high temperatures, low temperatures, humidity, and direct sunlight.

7. Control the storage temperature between 5°C and 35°C, keep relative humidity ≤60%, and store away from flammable, explosive, and corrosive items.

8. During storage, disconnect the robot from the charging dock; do not leave it plugged in for long periods to prevent battery damage from overcharging.

9. Store accessories separately in sealed bags with desiccant to prevent moisture absorption, mold, and aging.

10. Apply a thin layer of silicone oil to the water tank seal ring before storage, and place it in a sealed bag to prevent aging and cracking.

11. For long-term storage (>1 month), recharge the robot every 1-2 months to bring the battery level to 80%-90% to avoid deep discharge.

12. During storage, keep the robot body flat; do not invert or lay it on its side to prevent internal component displacement and battery leakage.

13. Avoid squeezing the robot with heavy objects during storage to prevent deformation of the body shell and accessories.

14. Store the charging dock separately from the robot; coil the power cord neatly and protect the plug to prevent dust ingress.

15. Before reusing the robot after long-term storage, first check if the battery is swollen or leaking, and if the body accessories are loose or aged.

16. Before reuse, thoroughly clean and dry all robot accessories, and replace expired or aged consumables.

17. Before reuse, power on the robot and let it run idle for 1-2 minutes to check for abnormal noises or stuttering, and test whether all functions are normal.

18. Before reuse, connect to the charging dock and charge for 30 minutes to activate the battery and calibrate the battery level display.

19. Before reuse, delete the old map, allow the robot to rebuild the map, and calibrate the sweeping/mopping, obstacle avoidance, and auto-recharge functions.

20. If the robot is stored in dark environments such as garages or storage rooms, disinfect the robot before storage and place insect-proof and rodent-proof accessories during storage.

## Fault Prevention Maintenance (20 items)

1. Regularly update the robot firmware and APP to ensure normal system functionality and reduce operational anomalies caused by software faults.

2. Avoid prolonged continuous operation of the robot; for large floor plans, divide cleaning into batches to prevent motor and battery overheating that leads to faults.

3. Before each use, check the floor for sharp hard objects, wires, ropes, etc., to avoid jamming mechanical parts or tangling accessories.

4. Strictly follow the model instructions for use; do not exceed weight limits, and avoid operating the robot in environments exceeding its cleaning area capacity.

5. Follow the "shallow charge, shallow discharge" principle when charging to avoid overcharging and deep discharging, thereby extending battery life and reducing battery faults.

6. Clean filters and dust bins promptly when blocked to avoid suction loss causing motor overload and potential motor burnout.

7. Immediately stop the machine if mopping cloths or water tanks leak, and clean up before resuming use to prevent water stains from entering the body and causing circuit short circuits.

8. If the robot exhibits slight abnormal noises or stuttering, immediately stop and inspect it; do not force usage to prevent fault expansion.

9. Avoid disassembling internal robot components (such as motors, motherboards, batteries) yourself; contact official after-sales service for repair in case of faults.

10. Conduct a comprehensive functional test of the robot monthly, including sweeping/mopping, obstacle avoidance, auto-recharge, and spot cleaning, to detect potential faults early.

11. Replace consumables promptly upon expiration to avoid work abnormalities caused by aged or worn consumables and damage to other accessories.

12. When connecting the robot to WiFi, use only the 2.4G network to avoid connection instability and functional anomalies caused by 5G networks.

13. Regularly clear the robot's cache (on the APP side) to avoid APP lag and command transmission failures due to excessive cache.

14. Avoid charging the robot in humid environments to prevent oxidation and short circuits of charging contacts, which lead to charging faults.

15. If the robot accidentally gets wet, immediately disconnect the power, place it in a ventilated area to dry, do not turn it on, and contact after-sales service for inspection.

16. Check the robot's ribbon cables and wires weekly for breakage or looseness to prevent faults caused by short circuits or poor contact.

17. For models with automatic mop washing, avoid dry burning in the cleaning chamber without water to prevent damage to the pump and heating module (if present).

18. For robots with dust collection systems, avoid continued use after the dust bag is full to prevent dust from entering the motor, which causes reduced suction and motor faults.

19. When the robot returns to charge, ensure there are no obstructions around the charging dock to prevent repeated charging attempts that cause wear on the motor and battery.

20. Establish a robot maintenance record, documenting consumable replacement times and fault handling situations, regularly summarize maintenance experience, and proactively prevent similar faults.
