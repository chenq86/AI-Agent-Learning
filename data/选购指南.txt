Buying Guide for Sweeping/Sweeping-and-Mopping Robots: 200 Tips

1. Core Selection Principle: Prioritize clarifying usage scenarios. For small apartments with single occupants, focus on lightweight and flexible models; for multi-person households with pets, emphasize suction power and anti-tangle features.

2. Suction Parameters: For home use, it is recommended to choose models with ≥3000Pa suction power. For carpeted areas, ≥4000Pa is required. Adjustable suction power is more suitable for different floor types.

3. Navigation Technology: Laser navigation offers precise positioning and strong interference resistance, making it suitable for complex layouts. Visual navigation provides better cost-effectiveness but should be avoided in strong light environments.

4. Obstacle Avoidance Capability: 3D structured light obstacle avoidance has higher recognition accuracy than infrared and can effectively avoid small debris. Households with pets should prioritize this configuration.

5. Battery Life: Small apartments (≤80㎡) require ≥60 minutes of runtime. Large apartments (≥120㎡) are recommended to have ≥120 minutes. Support for resume cleaning after interruption is more practical.

6. Water Tank Capacity: A clean water tank ≥300ml and a dirty water tank ≥250ml are suitable for large apartments. For small apartments, a capacity of around 200ml can be chosen to reduce body weight.

7. Mop Type: Electric rotating mops offer superior cleaning power compared to static pressure mops and are essential for stubborn stain scenarios. For wooden floors, it is recommended to choose models with lift-up mop functions.

8. Dust Collection Function: Automatic dust collection models are the top choice for those seeking convenience. A dust bag capacity ≥2.5L reduces replacement frequency. Pay attention to selecting designs with sealed, leak-proof dust containment.

9. Automatic Mop Washing: Prioritize models with hot air drying to prevent mold and odors on mops. Cleaning rotation speed ≥150 rpm ensures more thorough washing.

10. Brand Selection: First-tier brands (Ecovacs, Roborock) offer more comprehensive quality control and after-sales service. For niche brands, confirm whether there are local repair outlets.

11. Body Height: If the height under beds/sofas is ≤8cm, choose models with a body height ≤7.5cm to avoid inability to reach cleaning dead zones.

12. Threshold Crossing Ability: For thresholds with height ≤2cm, choose models capable of crossing obstacles ≥2cm. For carpet scenarios, enhanced drive wheel power is needed to avoid getting stuck.

13. Filtration System: HEPA filter grade ≥H13 can filter fine dust and allergens, which is essential for households with allergy-prone members. Note whether the filter is washable.

14. Main Brush Design: Anti-tangle main brushes (with silicone strips) are suitable for households with long hair or pets. Brushless designs are suitable for short-pile carpets and hard floors.

15. Side Brush Configuration: Dual side brushes provide better coverage than single side brushes, ensuring more thorough corner cleaning. Models with adjustable rotation speeds are suitable for different floor types.

16. APP Functions: Basic functions include zone cleaning, no-go area settings, and scheduled tasks. Advanced options may include voice control and multi-floor maps.

17. Network Compatibility: Confirm support for dual-band WiFi (2.4G/5G). For models supporting only 2.4G, ensure router signal coverage has no dead zones.

18. Noise Control: Operating noise ≤55dB is more suitable for bedroom use. Noise levels increase in carpet mode; prioritize models with noise-reducing motors.

19. Battery Type: Lithium batteries offer better battery life and longevity than nickel-cadmium batteries. Capacity ≥5200mAh is suitable for large apartments. Support for fast charging adds convenience.

20. Charging Efficiency: Fast charging mode (full charge within 2 hours) is suitable for frequent use. Standard charging (3-4 hours) offers better cost-effectiveness.

21. Material Selection: Prioritize ABS wear-resistant material for the body shell. Choose stainless steel for mop brackets to prevent rust. Select food-grade silicone for sealing rings.

22. Cleaning Modes: Essential modes include automatic, edge-following, and spot cleaning. Advanced options include carpet boost, silent, and precision cleaning modes to suit different scenarios.

23. Dirty Water Tank Design: Float ball sensors prevent dirty water backflow. Easily detachable and washable designs are more hassle-free and help avoid residual odors.

24. Water tank seal: Choose a design with double sealing rings to prevent water leakage from damaging internal components; test by tilting the body during trial use.

25. Voice control: Supports mainstream voice assistants such as Xiao Ai Tongxue and Tmall Genie for more convenient hands-free operation; verify voice recognition accuracy.

26. Multi-floor maps: Essential for duplex layouts; can store ≥3 maps, ensuring precise positioning without drift when switching floors.

27. Child lock function: Families with children should enable the child lock to prevent accidental operations; ensure it can be set via both body buttons and the APP.

28. After-sales policy: Prioritize models with whole-machine warranty ≥2 years and core component (motor, battery) warranty ≥3 years; after-sales response time ≤24 hours.

29. Accessory cost: Confirm prices for consumables like filters, mop cloths, and side brushes; choose models with high compatibility and cost-effectiveness to reduce long-term usage costs.

30. Weight control: A body weight ≤5kg is easier for manual lifting, making cleaning the charging dock and moving between floors less strenuous.

31. Floor adaptation: For wooden floors, choose models with liftable mop cloths and adjustable water output; for tile floors, prioritize strong suction + rotating mop combinations.

32. Pet-specific features: Households with pets should pay extra attention to anti-hair tangling and deodorizing functions; consider models with pet modes to enhance hair cleaning.

33. Dust handling: In scenarios with fine dust (e.g., after renovation), choose sealed dust bins + high-efficiency filtration to avoid secondary dust dispersion.

34. Ease of operation: For elderly users, prioritize models with simple buttons and intuitive APP interfaces, avoiding complex settings.

35. Firmware updates: Supports OTA online upgrades to optimize navigation algorithms and add new features, ensuring iterative improvements in long-term user experience.

36. Charging dock design: Charging contacts feature anti-oxidation coating; non-slip base prevents shifting; supports auto-recharge accuracy ≥95%.

37. Body buttons: Equipped with at least power on, return-to-charge, and quick-clean shortcuts; clear button feedback; waterproof design facilitates body cleaning.

38. Anti-drop function: Bottom has ≥4 sets of anti-drop sensors with wide detection range to prevent falling damage from stairs or high platforms.

39. Drive wheel material: Rubber material offers better slip resistance than plastic; textured design enhances grip, suitable for carpets and slippery floors.

40. Caster wheel design: Rotates 360° smoothly without sticking; includes an anti-tangle cover to prevent hair entanglement affecting steering.

41. Cleaning coverage rate: Refer to measured coverage rate ≥98% when purchasing to avoid missing large areas; prioritize planned cleaning models.

42. Water output adjustment: At least 3 levels of adjustment; low level for wooden floors, medium for tiles, high for stubborn stains, precisely adapting to scenarios.

43. Cleaning solution compatibility: Supports adding dedicated cleaning solutions; avoid using highly corrosive cleaners; some models feature automatic cleaning solution mixing.

44. Body waterproofing: Body waterproof rating ≥IPX4, capable of handling wet floors and minor splashes, preventing short circuits from water ingress.

45. Heat dissipation design: Motor heat dissipation holes are positioned reasonably to avoid blockage by debris; prevents overheating during prolonged work, protecting motor lifespan.

46. Map editing: Supports manually drawing cleaning zones, deleting erroneous maps, and adjusting no-go zone ranges, adapting to complex layouts.

47. Resume cleaning: Can resume cleaning progress after recharging following battery depletion, avoiding repeated cleaning and saving time and power.

49. Appearance Design: The body color should match the home style. Simple matte materials are dirt-resistant and easy to clean; avoid high-gloss materials that show fingerprints.
